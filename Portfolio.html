About

I chose computer science in the 11th grade, and it changed how I think. Python and MySQL gave me a foundation in programming, but the bigger lesson was logic: break a problem down, test each piece, and keep going until it works.

Today I’m at VIT Vellore, sharpening my technical skills and exploring the different corners of the field. I haven’t decided whether to stay in core computer science or specialise in a related area, and I’m treating that as a feature, not a gap.

Outside the code, I write to explain my thinking clearly. That habit turns out to matter as much in engineering as it does in English class.
